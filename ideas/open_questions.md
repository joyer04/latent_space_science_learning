# Open Questions

This file collects research questions that feel worth revisiting while reading and experimenting. They are prompts for learning, not claims that the answers are already known.

## Core Questions

- Do latent spaces contain causal structure, or do they primarily reflect statistical correlation?
- Can semantic axes be discovered without supervision?
- Is there a meaningful notion of geometry in representation spaces, or is geometry mostly an analysis convenience?
- How can we explore latent spaces without relying on downstream tasks as the main evaluation method?

## Additional Directions

- Which structures appear consistently across different architectures or training objectives?
- When a representation seems interpretable, what exactly is being interpreted: a feature, a direction, a local chart, or a projection artifact?
- How stable are latent structures across random seeds, scaling regimes, and fine-tuning steps?
- Can exploratory tools distinguish between human-readable structure and structure that is only statistically useful to the model?
- What kinds of probes reveal genuine organization without imposing too much of their own bias?
- Are there useful ways to study representation spaces that combine geometry, information theory, and dynamical systems perspectives?

## Working Attitude

These questions are here to keep the project exploratory. A good outcome is not a final answer, but a better way to look.
